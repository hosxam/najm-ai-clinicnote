Medical Report Draft Generator

ClinicNote helps clinicians structure de-identified notes into report drafts for review. It does not create official, legal, certified, insurance-approved, or fitness determinations.

FAQ

Is this an official medical report?

No. It creates a documentation draft only and requires clinician review, editing, and approval before use.

Does it make legal or insurance determinations?

No. It does not make legal, insurance, work, school, fitness, or certification decisions.

Do I need to log in?

No. The current site does not require login.

Does it store patient data?

No. It is intended for de-identified browser-based use and does not provide backend patient storage.

Can it diagnose or recommend treatment?

No. It structures clinician-provided details only.
