Pediatric SOAP Note Generator

Create pediatric SOAP note drafts from doctor-entered de-identified details, including parent or guardian advice, hydration status, relevant negatives, impression, plan, and follow-up.

Pediatric workflowsGuardian advice documentedNo patient identifiersReview required

Who this is for

Clinicians documenting pediatric outpatient visits such as fever, cough, vomiting, diarrhea, rash, feeding concerns, or follow-up visits.

What it generates

  • Subjective symptoms and relevant negatives
  • Objective hydration or examination notes
  • Doctor-entered assessment
  • Parent or guardian advice as documented
  • Follow-up and return precautions when entered

Fictional example

Fictional and de-identified.

Clinician-entered input

Pediatric fever with reduced activity. No breathing difficulty. No seizure reported. Urine output maintained. Hydration adequate. Impression: Viral febrile illness. Plan: parent/guardian advice given, hydration encouraged. Follow-up arranged.

Generated SOAP output example

SUBJECTIVE:
Fever with reduced activity.
Pertinent negatives: no breathing difficulty, no seizure reported.

OBJECTIVE:
Urine output maintained. Hydration adequate.

ASSESSMENT:
Viral febrile illness.

PLAN:
Parent/guardian advice given. Hydration encouraged. Follow-up arranged.

FAQ

Is this free?

Yes. ClinicNote is free to use in the browser.

Does it diagnose children?

No. It structures doctor-entered information only.

Can I enter patient identifiers?

No. Do not enter names, MRNs, phone numbers, dates of birth, addresses, or other identifiers.

Does it store pediatric notes?

No. It does not provide backend storage.

Does a clinician need to review it?

Yes. Outputs are drafts and require clinician review before use.
