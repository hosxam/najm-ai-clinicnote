Patient Instructions Generator for Doctors

ClinicNote helps clinicians turn doctor-entered plans, advice, and follow-up details into patient instruction drafts for review. It does not invent medications, dosing, diagnoses, or treatment plans.

Doctor-entered planNo invented treatmentNo storageClinician review required

Who this is for

Doctors who want a clear instruction draft based on their own documented plan and follow-up advice.

What it generates

  • Diagnosis or impression as entered by the doctor
  • Doctor advice / plan
  • Additional advice
  • Return precautions when entered
  • Follow-up timing

FAQ

Is this free?

Yes. ClinicNote is free to use in the browser.

Does it recommend treatment?

No. It structures the doctor-entered plan and does not recommend treatment.

Does it include medication dosing?

Only if the clinician enters it. ClinicNote does not invent medications or dosing.

Does ClinicNote store patient data?

No. It does not provide backend storage and should be used with de-identified details only.

Do outputs need review?

Yes. A licensed clinician must review and approve outputs before use.
