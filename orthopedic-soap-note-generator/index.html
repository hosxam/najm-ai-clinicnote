Orthopedic SOAP Note Generator

Create orthopedic and musculoskeletal SOAP note drafts from doctor-entered de-identified clinical details, including symptoms, relevant negatives, examination findings, investigations, impression, and plan.

MSK workflowsDoctor-entered findingsNo patient identifiersReview required

Who this is for

Doctors documenting orthopedic or musculoskeletal outpatient visits such as back pain, knee pain, shoulder pain, neck pain, or follow-up reviews.

What it generates

  • Subjective MSK complaint details
  • Pertinent negatives selected by the clinician
  • Exam findings documented by the clinician
  • Investigations reviewed
  • Doctor-entered impression and plan

Fictional example

Fictional and de-identified.

Clinician-entered input

Lower back pain, worse with movement. No bowel or bladder symptoms. No saddle anesthesia. No fever. Gait documented. Lumbar tenderness. Impression: Mechanical low back pain. Plan: activity modification advised and physiotherapy referral given.

Generated SOAP output example

SUBJECTIVE:
Lower back pain, worse with movement.
Pertinent negatives: no bowel/bladder symptoms, no saddle anesthesia, no fever.

OBJECTIVE:
Gait documented. Lumbar tenderness.

ASSESSMENT:
Mechanical low back pain.

PLAN:
Activity modification advised. Physiotherapy referral given. Follow-up arranged.

FAQ

Is this for orthopedic documentation?

Yes, it is a documentation draft workflow for doctor-entered orthopedic and MSK details.

Does it diagnose back pain?

No. The clinician enters the impression. ClinicNote does not diagnose.

Does it recommend treatment?

No. It structures the doctor-entered plan only.

Does it store patient data?

No. It is intended for de-identified browser-based use.

Is review required?

Yes. A licensed clinician must review the draft before use.
