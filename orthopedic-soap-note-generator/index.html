How it works: MSK documentation workflow

ClinicNote uses a chip-based input system tailored for musculoskeletal documentation. When you select an orthopedic workflow (for example, "knee pain" or "shoulder pain"), the tool presents pre-built chips organized by SOAP section and relevant to that joint or region.

For the Subjective section, you select symptom chips covering the presenting complaint, mechanism of injury, aggravating and relieving factors, and functional impact. Relevant negative chips include red flag symptoms such as "no night pain," "no constitutional symptoms," "no bowel or bladder dysfunction," and "no progressive neurological deficit." These negatives are critical in MSK documentation for ruling out serious pathology.

The Objective section is where orthopedic documentation becomes most detailed. Chips cover inspection findings (swelling, deformity, bruising), palpation (point tenderness, joint line tenderness), range of motion (full, limited, painful arc), and special tests. For the knee, this includes Lachman test, McMurray test, anterior drawer, and valgus/varus stress. For the shoulder, you can document Hawkins-Kennedy, Neer impingement, empty can test, and apprehension test results. The Ottawa ankle and knee rules can be documented as satisfied or not satisfied.

For Assessment, you enter your clinical impression. The Plan section offers management chips covering activity modification, analgesia, physiotherapy referral, imaging requests, orthotics, injection documentation, and follow-up timing. The entire workflow takes under 90 seconds for a standard MSK consultation and produces a structured note ready for EMR transfer.

When to use orthopedic SOAP format

Orthopedic and MSK consultations benefit from SOAP format because of the clear separation between patient-reported symptoms and clinician-observed examination findings. This distinction is particularly important in MSK medicine where the subjective complaint (pain, stiffness, instability) must be correlated with objective examination findings (ROM limitation, positive special tests, imaging results) to support the clinical impression.

SOAP format is well-suited for initial MSK consultations where you need to document a complete examination, follow-up visits tracking rehabilitation progress, post-operative reviews documenting wound healing and functional recovery, and sports medicine encounters where return-to-play decisions require clear documentation of functional status.

In the UAE and Gulf region, sports injuries represent a significant portion of orthopedic presentations. Football (soccer) injuries, running injuries related to desert marathons and outdoor training, and gym-related injuries are common. Proper documentation of mechanism, examination findings, and management plan is essential for insurance claims, employer fitness reports, and medicolegal purposes.

Post-operative documentation requires structured recording of wound status, drain output if relevant, neurovascular status, weight-bearing instructions, and rehabilitation milestones. The SOAP format keeps these elements organized and ensures nothing is missed during busy post-op clinic sessions. ClinicNote's chip system prompts you for each relevant element rather than relying on memory alone.
