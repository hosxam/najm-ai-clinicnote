Privacy

Privacy-first by design

ClinicNote is built for de-identified drafting and clinician control. It is designed to avoid patient data collection and unnecessary data transfer.

No login required

Core drafting workflows are available without creating a user account.

No patient data storage

No backend patient record storage is provided by ClinicNote.

No audio recording

No consultation recording or transcription flow is included.

Browser-based drafting

Drafting runs in-browser and copied output remains clinician managed.

Safe feedback links

Feedback actions route to external forms with safe open behavior.

De-identification reminder

Never enter names, MRNs, numbers, addresses, or direct identifiers.

Important: ClinicNote is for de-identified clinical drafting only. A licensed clinician must review and approve every final output before use.
