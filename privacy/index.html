Privacy

Privacy

ClinicNote is designed for browser-based, de-identified documentation drafting. Do not enter patient identifiers.

No login or account

ClinicNote does not require a login or user account.

No audio recording

ClinicNote does not record consultations, upload audio, or transcribe audio.

No patient data storage

The current site does not include backend patient data storage. It is intended for de-identified doctor-entered details only.

Do not enter patient identifiers

Do not enter names, MRNs, phone numbers, exact dates of birth, Emirates ID, addresses, insurance IDs, or other patient identifiers.

Clinician review required

All outputs are drafts and require review, editing, and approval by a licensed clinician before use.
