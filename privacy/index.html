Privacy

Privacy

ClinicNote is designed for browser-based, de-identified documentation drafting. Do not enter patient identifiers.

What ClinicNote does not collect

✖ No login or account
✖ No patient names
✖ No dates of birth
✖ No phone numbers
✖ No email addresses
✖ No MRN / IDs
✖ No audio recording
✖ No patient data storage
✖ No backend submission
✖ No analytics tracking

How it works

ClinicNote runs entirely in your browser. You enter de-identified clinical details, select from available prompts, and generate a structured draft. Nothing is uploaded, stored, or transmitted to any server.

Clinician review required

All generated drafts require review, editing, and approval by a licensed clinician before clinical use.
