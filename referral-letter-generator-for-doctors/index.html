Referral Letter Generator for Doctors

ClinicNote helps doctors structure de-identified referral information into a referral letter draft. The clinician controls the reason, history, findings, impression, current plan, and requested action.

Referral draftDoctor-entered impressionNo patient identifiersReview required

Who this is for

Clinicians who need a structured referral draft after documenting a patient encounter with de-identified details.

What it generates

  • Reason for referral
  • Relevant history
  • Exam findings and investigations
  • Doctor-entered impression
  • Current plan and requested action

Fictional example

Fictional and de-identified.

Clinician-entered input

Lower back pain worse with movement. No bowel or bladder symptoms. No saddle anesthesia. Gait documented. Lumbar tenderness. Impression: Mechanical low back pain. Referral request: physiotherapy assessment.

Generated referral output example

REFERRAL LETTER
Reason for referral: lower back pain with activity limitation
History: lower back pain worse with movement
Pertinent negatives: no bowel/bladder symptoms, no saddle anesthesia
Exam findings: gait documented, lumbar tenderness
Working impression: Mechanical low back pain
Current plan/request: physiotherapy assessment
Safety and privacy note: This is a documentation draft only. Do not enter patient identifiers. Do not use it as an official or certified report without clinician review and approval.

FAQ

Is this free?

Yes. ClinicNote is free to use in the browser.

Does it store referral details?

No. The tool does not provide backend patient data storage.

Can I enter patient identifiers?

No. Use de-identified details only.

Does it decide referral urgency?

No. It structures doctor-entered details and does not make clinical, insurance, or legal determinations.

Does a clinician need to review it?

Yes. All outputs are drafts and require clinician review before use.
