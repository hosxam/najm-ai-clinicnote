# Help Test Najm AI ClinicNote

Najm AI ClinicNote is a free no-login clinical documentation tool for doctors. It helps structure de-identified doctor-entered information into OPD notes, referrals, patient instructions, and report drafts.

## Safety Note

Do not enter real patient identifiers or real patient data.

Use fictional or de-identified details only. Do not enter:

- patient names
- MRNs
- phone numbers
- exact dates of birth
- addresses
- Emirates ID
- insurance IDs
- real clinical notes from a patient encounter
- consultation recordings

All generated outputs are drafts for clinician review.

## Testing Steps

1. Open the website.
2. Try OPD Note Builder.
3. Search a workflow, such as `diabetes`, `fever`, or `back pain`.
4. Review the preselected chips.
5. Untick anything that does not apply.
6. Add one custom detail.
7. Generate the note.
8. Check EMR, SOAP, and Instructions.
9. Try Medical Report Draft with fictional or de-identified notes.
10. Submit feedback.

## What To Notice

While testing, please notice:

- Did it save time?
- Was the output useful?
- Was anything missing?
- Did the defaults feel helpful or overselected?
- Could you untick defaults easily?
- Did custom entries work?
- What would make you use it again?
- What specialty or workflow should be added next?

## Feedback Questions

Please answer these without patient information:

- Did it save time?
- Was the output useful enough to paste into an EMR after review?
- What was missing?
- What wording felt awkward?
- What would make you use it again?
- What specialty/workflow should be added next?
- Would you share this with another doctor?

## Reminder

ClinicNote does not replace clinical judgment. Review, edit, and approve every draft before use.
