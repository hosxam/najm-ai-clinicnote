Dermatology SOAP Note Generator

Create dermatology SOAP note drafts from doctor-entered de-identified details such as rash symptoms, distribution, morphology if assessed, relevant negatives, clinician impression, plan, and follow-up.

Dermatology workflowsDoctor-entered findingsNo patient identifiersReview required

Who this is for

Doctors documenting dermatology outpatient visits such as rash, itching, acne, eczema, fungal infections, or wound review.

What it generates

  • Subjective skin complaint details
  • Relevant negatives selected by the clinician
  • Exam findings such as distribution or morphology if assessed
  • Doctor-entered impression and plan
  • Follow-up details

Fictional example

Fictional and de-identified.

Clinician-entered input

Rash with itching. Duration and distribution documented. No breathing difficulty reported. No mucosal involvement reported. Morphology reviewed. Impression: Dermatitis-type rash. Plan: skin care advice given. Follow-up arranged.

Generated SOAP output example

SUBJECTIVE:
Rash with itching. Duration documented.
Pertinent negatives: no breathing difficulty reported, no mucosal involvement reported.

OBJECTIVE:
Distribution documented. Morphology reviewed.

ASSESSMENT:
Dermatitis-type rash.

PLAN:
Skin care advice given. Follow-up arranged.
Safety and privacy note: Use de-identified doctor-entered details only. This page does not diagnose skin conditions or recommend treatment. Clinician review is required.

FAQ

Is this free?

Yes. ClinicNote is free to use in the browser.

Does it diagnose rashes?

No. The clinician enters the impression. ClinicNote does not diagnose.

Does it recommend treatment?

No. It structures the doctor-entered plan only.

Does it store photos or patient data?

No. ClinicNote does not provide image upload or backend patient storage.

Is clinician review required?

Yes. All outputs are drafts and require clinician review before use.
