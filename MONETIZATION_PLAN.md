# Najm AI ClinicNote — Monetization Plan

## Pricing Tiers

### Free MVP (launch at $0)
The full MVP is free. No paywalls, no trial, no credit card. All features are available immediately:
- OPD Note Builder with all 6 specialties + general follow-up
- Visit-type presets with documentation prompts
- Referral Builder (3 formats: formal, short, handover)
- Patient Instructions Generator (English, Arabic, WhatsApp-style)
- Privacy-first stateless workflow

This builds trust and adoption. Doctors who use it daily will want more.

### Early Access Lifetime ($19 one-time)
One-time payment for all future Pro features. This is the primary monetization vehicle for now.

### Pro Subscription ($5/month in future)
When Pro features are built:
- Additional specialties (cardiology, neurology, psych, ophtho)
- PDF and Word export with clean formatting
- Clinic branding on outputs
- Saved custom templates (local storage only, no cloud)
- Advanced documentation prompts
- Post-op and chronic disease follow-up templates
- Medico-legal documentation checklist
- Arabic/English bilingual outputs

## Revenue Model

| Tier | Price | Est. Conversion | Revenue per 100 visitors |
|------|-------|----------------|--------------------------|
| Free MVP | $0 | 100% | $0 (adoption) |
| Early access | $19 | 3-5% of active users | $57-$95 |
| Pro (future) | $5/mo | 1-2% of active users | $5-$10/mo |

## Go-to-Market Strategy

### Phase 1: Adoption (MVP launch)
- List on Product Hunt
- Share in medical Facebook groups
- Post on LinkedIn about the build process
- Collect email signups via a simple waitlist form on homepage

### Phase 2: Validation (first 90 days)
- Target: 200 active weekly users
- Target: 50 early access purchases ($950)
- Collect feedback on missing features
- Identify top-requested specialties

### Phase 3: Monetization (90-180 days)
- Build top 3 requested Pro features
- Launch Pro tier at $5/month
- Grandfather early access users into lifetime Pro

### Phase 4: Expansion (180+ days)
- Build bilingual Arabic/English mode
- Add clinic branding as a paid add-on
- Explore partnerships with clinic management software

## Cost Structure

The MVP runs entirely in-browser with no server costs. Future costs:
- Domain: ~$10/year
- GitHub Pages: free (for now)
- PDF generation library: open source (pdf-lib)
- Total monthly operating cost: $0-$10

## Why Free First?

The OPD documentation market is crowded with paid EMRs and AI scribes. Doctors are skeptical of new tools. Free removes the adoption barrier. Once ClinicNote is part of their daily workflow, upgrading to Pro for $5/month is a small ask relative to the time saved.
