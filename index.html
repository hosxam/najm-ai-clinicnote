Fictional Examples

These examples are fictional and de-identified. They show the kind of doctor-entered input ClinicNote can structure for clinician review.

Fever / URTI

Fictional and de-identified

Clinician-entered input

Adult with 3 days fever, dry cough, sore throat, nasal congestion, and myalgia. Autofill contributed respiratory red flag negatives: no shortness of breath, no chest pain, no confusion, no neck stiffness, no persistent vomiting. Temperature, throat, and chest exam findings entered by clinician. Impression: Viral URTI. Plan: supportive care discussed, fluids encouraged, red flags explained, follow-up if worsening.

Generated EMR output

Chief concern: Fever / URTI symptoms for 3 days.

History: Fever with dry cough, sore throat, nasal congestion, and myalgia. Autofill prompted red flag review for respiratory distress, chest pain, confusion, neck stiffness, and persistent vomiting; clinician documented the applicable negatives.

Examination: Temperature recorded. Throat examination findings entered. Chest auscultation entered.

Impression: Viral URTI (clinician-entered).

Plan: Supportive care discussed. Fluids encouraged. Antipyretic use reviewed by clinician. Return precautions given. Follow-up advised if symptoms worsen or new concerning symptoms develop.

Safety-netting: Red flags explained to patient.

Diabetes follow-up

Fictional and de-identified

Clinician-entered input

Routine type 2 diabetes follow-up. Autofill prompted review of glucose log, hypoglycemia episodes, medication adherence, foot symptoms, BP, HbA1c, renal profile, lipid panel, and screening status. Clinician entered: home readings reviewed, no hypoglycemia symptoms, no foot ulcers, monofilament sensation intact, HbA1c: [clinician-entered value], BP reviewed. Impression: Diabetes follow-up, stable. Plan: medication adherence reinforced, lifestyle counseling discussed, follow-up in 3 months.

Generated SOAP output

SUBJECTIVE: Routine type 2 diabetes follow-up. Home glucose readings reviewed. No hypoglycemia symptoms reported. No foot ulcer symptoms reported. Medication adherence reviewed by clinician.

OBJECTIVE: Foot inspection documented. Monofilament sensation intact. BP reviewed. HbA1c reviewed and discussed by clinician. Autofill prompted renal function, lipid panel, and screening status for clinician review.

ASSESSMENT: Type 2 diabetes follow-up, stable (clinician-entered).

PLAN: Medication adherence reinforced. Lifestyle counseling discussed. Diabetes screening status reviewed. Follow-up arranged in 3 months.

Low back pain

Fictional and de-identified

Clinician-entered input

Adult with 1 week lower back pain after lifting, worse with movement and prolonged sitting. Autofill prompted MSK red flag review: no bowel or bladder symptoms, no saddle anesthesia, no fever, no trauma, no progressive weakness. Gait, lumbar tenderness, and lower-limb neurovascular status documented by clinician. Impression: Mechanical low back pain. Plan: activity modification discussed, analgesia reviewed, physiotherapy referral arranged, red flags explained, follow-up arranged.

Generated EMR output

Chief concern: Lower back pain for 1 week.

History: Pain began after lifting and is worse with movement and prolonged sitting. Autofill prompted red flag review; clinician documented no bowel/bladder symptoms, no saddle anesthesia, no fever, no trauma, and no progressive weakness.

Examination: Gait documented. Lumbar spine tenderness elicited. Lower-limb power, sensation, and pulses documented by clinician.

Impression: Mechanical low back pain (clinician-entered).

Plan: Activity modification discussed. Analgesia reviewed by clinician. Physiotherapy referral arranged. Red flags explained. Follow-up arranged.

Safety-netting: Worsening symptoms, new neurological symptoms, or persistent symptoms beyond expected timeframe to trigger re-evaluation.

Pediatric fever

Fictional and de-identified

    Privacy

    How ClinicNote is designed to avoid patient data collection.

    No login, no account, no backend storage

    ClinicNote runs in the browser as a client-side documentation assistant. It does not require a login and does not provide user accounts, cloud storage, audio capture, or patient record storage.

    De-identified use only

    Users should enter de-identified clinical details only. Do not enter names, MRNs, phone numbers, exact dates of birth, Emirates ID, addresses, or other patient identifiers.

    Browser-based workflow

    The tool structures doctor-entered information in the browser. Any text you copy from ClinicNote is handled by your browser and remains under your control.

    No analytics at this stage

    This public-readiness phase does not add analytics, tracking, login, backend submission, audio recording, or persistent data storage.

    Clinician responsibility

    All generated outputs are drafts and require review, editing, and approval by a licensed clinician before use.

    About Najm AI

    Najm AI is a healthtech brand focused on building practical, safe, and accessible tools for healthcare professionals. We believe in technology that empowers clinicians without replacing their judgment or compromising patient privacy.

    ClinicNote

    ClinicNote is a browser-based documentation assistance tool that helps clinicians structure de-identified, doctor-entered details into standard formats like SOAP notes, short EMR entries, referral letters, patient instructions, and report drafts. It is not a full AI scribe. It does not use audio recording or generate notes from conversation. Instead, it provides a structured interface where doctors enter or select their own clinical details and review every output before use.

    What ClinicNote Is

    • A structured documentation assistant that organizes doctor-entered notes
    • A productivity tool that reduces time spent on routine documentation
    • A safety-focused application that operates entirely client-side
    • An educational tool for understanding clinical documentation formats

    What ClinicNote Is Not

    • Not a medical device or clinical decision support system
    • Not an AI scribe that listens to or transcribes consultations
    • Not a replacement for clinical judgment or documentation
    • Not a data storage or transmission platform
    • Not FDA-cleared, CE-marked, or DHA-approved

    Safety Commitment

    Protecting patient privacy is our top priority. ClinicNote is designed from the ground up with safety as a core principle, not an afterthought. Every design decision prioritizes clinician control and patient data protection.

    Safety

    Non-Negotiable Rules

    • No patient-identifiable information (PHI) may be entered into ClinicNote
    • No audio recording or transcription of any kind
    • No data is stored, transmitted, or shared by ClinicNote
    • No clinical decision support or diagnostic suggestions
    • No treatment recommendations or medication advice
    • All outputs must be reviewed and approved by a licensed clinician before use
    Disclaimer: Najm AI ClinicNote is an educational/productivity documentation assistant. It is not a medical device, not clinical decision support, and not a replacement for clinician judgment. All clinical content must be verified and approved by a licensed healthcare professional.

    Data Handling

    ClinicNote operates entirely within your browser. No data is sent to any server, stored in any database, or accessible by Najm AI or any third party. When you close or refresh the page, all entered data is cleared. The application has no backend, no API, and no persistent storage mechanism.

    Any data you copy from ClinicNote is handled by your browser standard clipboard functionality and remains under your control.

    Regulatory Status

    ClinicNote by Najm AI is a documentation productivity tool. It is:

    • Not FDA-cleared or FDA-approved
    • Not CE-marked under medical device regulations
    • Not DHA-approved as a medical device
    • Not intended for use in clinical decision making

    ClinicNote is designed for educational and productivity purposes only. It does not interpret clinical data, provide diagnoses, recommend treatments, or replace any aspect of clinical practice.
