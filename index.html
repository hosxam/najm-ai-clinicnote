Fictional Examples

These examples are fictional and de-identified. They show the kind of doctor-entered input ClinicNote can structure for clinician review.

Fever / URTI

Fictional and de-identified

Clinician-entered input

Adult with 3 days fever, dry cough, sore throat, nasal congestion, and myalgia. Autofill added relevant negatives: no shortness of breath, no chest pain, no confusion, no neck stiffness, no persistent vomiting. Temperature and throat/chest exam documented by clinician. Impression: Viral URTI. Plan entered by clinician: supportive care, fluids, antipyretic plan, red flags explained, follow-up if worsening or not improving.

Generated EMR output

Chief concern: Fever / URTI symptoms for 3 days.

History: Fever with dry cough, sore throat, nasal congestion, and myalgia. Autofill contributed relevant negatives for respiratory distress, chest pain, confusion, neck stiffness, and persistent vomiting; clinician kept the applicable items.

Examination documented: Temperature recorded. Throat examination documented. Chest examination documented by clinician.

Clinician-entered impression: Viral URTI.

Clinician-entered plan: Supportive care discussed, fluids encouraged, antipyretic plan documented by clinician.

Safety-netting / follow-up: Red flags explained. Follow-up advised if symptoms worsen, persist, or new concerning symptoms develop.

Diabetes follow-up

Fictional and de-identified

Clinician-entered input

Routine type 2 diabetes follow-up. Autofill added review prompts for glucose log, hypoglycemia, medication adherence, foot symptoms, BP, HbA1c, renal profile, lipid review, and screening status. Clinician entered: home readings reviewed, no hypoglycemia symptoms, no foot ulcers, monofilament sensation documented, HbA1c: [clinician-entered value], BP reviewed. Impression: Diabetes follow-up, stable. Plan entered by clinician: medication review completed, lifestyle counseling reinforced, follow-up in 3 months.

Generated SOAP output

SUBJECTIVE: Routine type 2 diabetes follow-up. Home glucose readings reviewed and discussed. No hypoglycemia symptoms reported. No foot ulcer symptoms reported. Medication adherence reviewed by clinician.

OBJECTIVE: Foot inspection documented. Monofilament sensation documented. BP reviewed. HbA1c: [clinician-entered value] reviewed and discussed by clinician. Autofill contributed renal/lipid/screening prompts for clinician review.

ASSESSMENT: Type 2 diabetes follow-up, stable by clinician impression.

PLAN: Medication review documented by clinician. Lifestyle counseling reinforced. Diabetes screening status reviewed where applicable. Follow-up arranged in 3 months.

    Privacy

    How ClinicNote is designed to avoid patient data collection.

    No login, no account, no backend storage

    ClinicNote runs in the browser as a client-side documentation assistant. It does not require a login and does not provide user accounts, cloud storage, audio capture, or patient record storage.

    De-identified use only

    Users should enter de-identified clinical details only. Do not enter names, MRNs, phone numbers, exact dates of birth, Emirates ID, addresses, or other patient identifiers.

    Browser-based workflow

    The tool structures doctor-entered information in the browser. Any text you copy from ClinicNote is handled by your browser and remains under your control.

    No analytics at this stage

    This public-readiness phase does not add analytics, tracking, login, backend submission, audio recording, or persistent data storage.

    Clinician responsibility

    All generated outputs are drafts and require review, editing, and approval by a licensed clinician before use.

    About Najm AI

    Najm AI is a healthtech brand focused on building practical, safe, and accessible tools for healthcare professionals. We believe in technology that empowers clinicians without replacing their judgment or compromising patient privacy.

    ClinicNote

    ClinicNote is a browser-based documentation assistance tool that helps clinicians structure de-identified, doctor-entered details into standard formats like SOAP notes, short EMR entries, referral letters, patient instructions, and report drafts. It is not a full AI scribe. It does not use audio recording or generate notes from conversation. Instead, it provides a structured interface where doctors enter or select their own clinical details and review every output before use.

    What ClinicNote Is

    • A structured documentation assistant that organizes doctor-entered notes
    • A productivity tool that reduces time spent on routine documentation
    • A safety-focused application that operates entirely client-side
    • An educational tool for understanding clinical documentation formats

    What ClinicNote Is Not

    • Not a medical device or clinical decision support system
    • Not an AI scribe that listens to or transcribes consultations
    • Not a replacement for clinical judgment or documentation
    • Not a data storage or transmission platform
    • Not FDA-cleared, CE-marked, or DHA-approved

    Protecting patient privacy is our top priority. ClinicNote is designed from the ground up with safety as a core principle, not an afterthought. Every design decision prioritizes clinician control and patient data protection.

    Safety

    Non-Negotiable Rules

    • No patient-identifiable information (PHI) may be entered into ClinicNote
    • No audio recording or transcription of any kind
    • No data is stored, transmitted, or shared by ClinicNote
    • No clinical decision support or diagnostic suggestions
    • No treatment recommendations or medication advice
    • All outputs must be reviewed and approved by a licensed clinician before use
    Disclaimer: Najm AI ClinicNote is an educational/productivity documentation assistant. It is not a medical device, not clinical decision support, and not a replacement for clinician judgment. All clinical content must be verified and approved by a licensed healthcare professional.

    Data Handling

    ClinicNote operates entirely within your browser. No data is sent to any server, stored in any database, or accessible by Najm AI or any third party. When you close or refresh the page, all entered data is cleared. The application has no backend, no API, and no persistent storage mechanism.

    Any data you copy from ClinicNote is handled by your browser standard clipboard functionality and remains under your control.
