# V4 Guideline Source Registry — Safety Position

## Date
2026-05-19

## Purpose
This registry tracks clinical guideline and reference sources for future internal review. It does NOT constitute guideline endorsement, treatment recommendation, or automatic plan generation.

## Safety Principles

1. **Source tracking only.** The registry documents which external clinical sources were reviewed or referenced during content development. It does not mean ClinicNote is guideline-approved, guideline-compliant, or endorsed by any issuing body.

2. **No treatment recommendations.** Source references do not imply that any specific plan, medication, or management option is recommended. All clinical content requires clinician review and local policy application.

3. **No automatic plan generation.** The registry is metadata. It is not wired to automatic decision support. No plan option, medication option, or calculator result will be auto-generated from registry entries.

4. **Clinician review required.** Every source-linked content item must be confirmed by a licensed clinician before use. Source references are documentation aids, not clinical directives.

5. **Local policy applies.** Guidelines referenced in this registry may not reflect local institutional policy, local drug formularies, or regional clinical protocols. The treating clinician determines appropriate management.

6. **Source verification required.** Before any registry entry influences clinical content, the source must be reviewed for currency (version, publication date, withdrawal status) and applicability to the intended clinical context.

7. **No endorsement claims.** ClinicNote does not claim NHS, NICE, DHA, MOHAP, or any other organizational endorsement. Source references are for transparency, not marketing.

8. **Deprecation policy.** Outdated or withdrawn guidelines must be marked `deprecated` and must not influence active content.
