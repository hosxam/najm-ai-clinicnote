Free SOAP Note Generator for Doctors

ClinicNote helps doctors turn doctor-entered de-identified clinical details into a structured SOAP note draft for clinician review. It is browser-based, requires no login, and does not use audio recording or patient data storage.

No loginNo audioNo storageClinician review required

Who this is for

This page is for doctors and clinical teams who want a quick way to structure de-identified consultation details into a SOAP note draft.

ClinicNote does not diagnose, recommend treatment, or replace clinician judgment. It structures what the clinician enters.

What it generates

  • Subjective section from doctor-entered symptoms and relevant negatives
  • Objective section from documented examination and investigations
  • Assessment from the doctor-entered impression
  • Plan from the doctor-entered plan and follow-up details

Clinician-entered input

Fever and cough for several days. Sore throat. No shortness of breath. No chest pain. Chest clear on auscultation. Impression: Viral URTI. Plan: supportive care, fluids encouraged, antipyretic advised. Follow-up if symptoms worsen.

Generated SOAP output example

SUBJECTIVE:
Fever, cough, sore throat. No shortness of breath. No chest pain.

OBJECTIVE:
Chest clear on auscultation.

ASSESSMENT:
Viral URTI.

PLAN:
Supportive care, fluids encouraged, antipyretic advised.
Follow-up if symptoms persist or worsen.
Safety and privacy note: Use de-identified doctor-entered details only. Do not enter names, MRNs, phone numbers, exact dates of birth, Emirates ID, addresses, or other patient identifiers. Outputs are documentation drafts and require clinician review before use.

FAQ

Is this free?

Yes. ClinicNote is available as a free browser-based documentation tool.

Do I need to log in?

No. The current site does not require login or accounts.

Does ClinicNote store patient data?

No. It is designed for de-identified use and does not provide backend patient data storage.

Can I use this for real clinical work?

Only as a documentation draft tool using de-identified details. A licensed clinician must review, edit, and approve any output before use.

Does it diagnose or recommend treatment?

No. It structures doctor-entered details and does not provide diagnosis or treatment advice.
