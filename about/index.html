About

Built for clinicians who need faster documentation

Najm AI ClinicNote is a browser-based drafting tool focused on speed, structure, and clear safety boundaries for clinician-authored notes.

What Najm AI ClinicNote is

  • A browser-based documentation assistant for de-identified clinician-entered details.
  • A workflow system for OPD notes, SOAP notes, referral drafts, instructions, and report drafts.
  • A productivity tool that keeps final review and approval with the clinician.

What it is not

  • Not a medical device.
  • Not a diagnostic engine.
  • Not autonomous treatment planning.
  • Not a patient-data storage platform.

Why it exists

Clinic workflows can be slowed by repetitive formatting and documentation overhead. ClinicNote exists to reduce that overhead while preserving clinician control and documentation clarity.

Future direction

Najm AI Scribe is a future product direction for research and pilot work. ClinicNote remains the current free documentation utility.

If you want updates, use the feedback page and select the Scribe waitlist option.
