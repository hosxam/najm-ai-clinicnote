About Najm AI ClinicNote

A free browser-based documentation assistant built by a doctor in training, for doctors and medical students who want clean structured drafts in seconds.

Who built it

ClinicNote is built by Hossam Abdelmageed, a medical intern at NMC Royal Hospital DIP, Dubai, UAE. The tool grew out of the everyday reality of OPD shifts: clinically obvious encounters slowed down by minutes of typing, abbreviations, and repeated structure. ClinicNote is what I wish I had on my own first rotation.

What ClinicNote is

ClinicNote is a free browser-based tool that helps doctors and medical students structure OPD notes, SOAP notes, referral letters, patient instructions, and medical report drafts. It runs entirely in the browser and uses pre-built workflows and specialty-specific prompts to organize doctor-entered clinical details into clean drafts.

Who it is for

  • Doctors who want to spend more of the consultation looking at the patient and less of it typing.
  • Medical students and interns who want a built-in framework so no history component gets forgotten during a presentation.
  • Clinic staff and locums who need fast, portable note structure without signing up for an EMR.

How it works

Pick a specialty and workflow. Tap the chips that match what you found. Add free-text where you need to. Click Generate. ClinicNote produces SOAP, EMR, follow-up, referral, and patient-instruction drafts from the same selection. Nothing is recorded, transcribed, or inferred. The clinician controls every input.

Privacy by design

  • No login or account required.
  • No audio recording or transcription.
  • No clinical text leaves your device.
  • De-identified information only.
  • Full disclosure of the small technical exceptions on the Privacy page.

Clinician review is required

Every draft generated by ClinicNote requires review by a licensed clinician before clinical use. The tool provides structure, not clinical judgment. It is not a medical device, not clinical decision support, and not a replacement for professional evaluation.

Scribe is the next step. If you want to know when it is live, you can join the Scribe update list. Email only, no spam, no patient data.

Where it works

ClinicNote supports English-language OPD documentation across geographic settings. The workflows cover common specialties relevant to outpatient practice in the UAE first, then MENA and globally. The tool does not target any specific healthcare system, regulatory framework, or licensing body. It is a general documentation assistant designed for individual clinician and student use.
