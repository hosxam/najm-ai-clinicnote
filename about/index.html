About Najm AI ClinicNote

ClinicNote is a practical documentation assistant for clinicians who want structured drafts from de-identified, doctor-entered clinical details.

What ClinicNote is

ClinicNote helps structure OPD notes, SOAP notes, referral drafts, patient instruction drafts, and medical report drafts. It is browser-based and designed for clinician control.

What ClinicNote is not

ClinicNote is not an AI scribe, not audio transcription, not a medical device, not clinical decision support, and not a replacement for clinician judgment.

Safety commitment

ClinicNote is intended for de-identified use only. All outputs require clinician review before use.
