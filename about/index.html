About Najm AI ClinicNote

A browser-based documentation assistant designed for doctors who want structured clinical drafts from their own de-identified notes.

What ClinicNote is

ClinicNote is a free browser-based tool that helps clinicians structure OPD notes, SOAP notes, referral letters, patient instructions, and medical report drafts. It runs entirely in the browser and uses pre-built workflows and specialty-specific prompts to organize doctor-entered clinical details into clean drafts.

Why it exists

ClinicNote was built because repetitive OPD documentation takes time away from patients. The goal is to give clinicians a fast, structured way to produce drafts from their own de-identified notes without leaving the browser, signing up for a platform, or relying on cloud processing.

It exists as a free utility first. The longer-term direction is Najm AI Scribe, which remains a future concept under exploration.

Doctor-controlled documentation

The clinician controls every part of the input. Nothing is transcribed, recorded, or inferred. You enter your own findings, select from available workflow chips, and structure the output yourself. ClinicNote does not generate clinical content or make decisions. It formats what you provide.

Privacy by design

  • No login or account required.
  • No audio recording or transcription.
  • No patient data is stored or transmitted.
  • Everything stays in the browser on your device.
  • Designed for de-identified clinical details only.

Clinician review is required

Every draft generated by ClinicNote requires review by a licensed clinician before clinical use. The tool provides structure, not clinical judgment. It is not a medical device, not clinical decision support, and not a replacement for professional evaluation.

Future direction: Najm AI Scribe

Najm AI Scribe is a future concept being explored. ClinicNote is what exists today and is available free. If you are interested in following future developments, including potential research or pilot opportunities, you can join the mailing list. There is no active Scribe product or timeline to announce.

Relevance for UAE, MENA, and global doctors

ClinicNote supports English-language OPD documentation workflows used across geographic settings. The workflows cover common specialties relevant to outpatient practice in the UAE, MENA region, and internationally. The tool does not target any specific healthcare system, regulatory framework, or licensing body. It is a general documentation assistant designed for individual clinician use.
