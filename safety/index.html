Safety

Safety

ClinicNote is a documentation drafting tool. It does not diagnose, recommend treatment, replace clinical judgment, or store patient data.

Non-negotiable rules

⚠ No patient identifiers
⚠ No audio recording
⚠ No diagnosis provided
⚠ No treatment advice
⚠ Drafts only
⚠ Clinician review required

What ClinicNote is

ClinicNote is a browser-based documentation drafting tool. It helps clinicians structure de-identified, doctor-entered details into standard clinical note formats. It does not generate clinical content, make diagnoses, or recommend treatments.

What ClinicNote is not

  • Not a medical device
  • Not clinical decision support
  • Not a substitute for clinical judgment
  • Not a replacement for your EMR
  • Not a patient data storage system
  • Not HIPAA-compliant or certified under any regulatory framework

Clinician responsibility

All outputs are drafts and require review, editing, and approval by a licensed clinician before use. The clinician retains full responsibility for all documentation created using this tool.
