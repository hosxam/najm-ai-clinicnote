Safety

Safety principles

ClinicNote supports doctor-controlled drafting workflows. It does not replace clinical judgment and must be reviewed by a licensed clinician before use.

Doctor-controlled drafts

Every output is a draft that remains under clinician review, editing, and final approval.

No diagnosis invention

ClinicNote does not autonomously diagnose or function as clinical decision support.

No autonomous treatment recommendations

It does not independently prescribe treatment plans or medication advice.

No patient identifiers

Do not enter names, MRNs, phone numbers, IDs, addresses, or other direct identifiers.

No storage

ClinicNote does not provide backend patient data storage.

Browser-first workflow

Drafting happens in-browser and copied outputs remain under clinician control.
