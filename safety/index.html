Safety

Safety

ClinicNote is a documentation drafting tool. It does not diagnose, recommend treatment, replace clinical judgment, or store patient data.

Non-negotiable rules

  • No patient-identifiable information should be entered.
  • No audio recording or transcription is used.
  • No diagnosis or treatment recommendations are provided.
  • Outputs are documentation drafts only.
  • Clinician review, editing, and approval are required before use.

Regulatory status

ClinicNote is not a medical device, not clinical decision support, and not a substitute for clinical judgment.
