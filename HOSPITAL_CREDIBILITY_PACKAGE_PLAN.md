# Hospital Credibility Package Plan

## Purpose
Plan future evidence assets for institutional conversations without claiming approval, deployment, certification, or regulatory compliance.

## Positioning
ClinicNote is currently a no-login, browser-based documentation utility for de-identified clinician-entered information. Any institutional review would require separate governance, clinical safety review, privacy review, legal review, and local policy alignment.

## Future Assets To Prepare

### Safety Framework
- Product safety boundaries.
- Do-not-cross clinical rules.
- Clinician-reviewed draft language.
- No diagnosis/treatment recommendation statement.
- Feature flag inventory for risky/internal functionality.

### Privacy Architecture
- No backend architecture summary.
- No login/account summary.
- No patient data storage.
- Local-only export behavior.
- No-PHI form and feedback policy.
- No clinical text analytics policy.

### No-PHI Analytics Summary
- Dry-run analytics schema.
- Allowed events and properties.
- Forbidden payloads.
- Evidence that notes, outputs, identifiers, and custom text are not collected.

### Doctor Feedback Summary
- Only after private testing occurs.
- Aggregate themes only.
- Remove and ignore any response containing patient data.
- Never copy patient information into reports, prompts, datasets, or issues.

### Usage Metrics Without Patient Data
- Page/tool usage counts only if a privacy-reviewed analytics provider is later approved.
- No clinical text.
- No generated output.
- No free-text values.
- No calculator values.

### Workflow Library Depth
- Count of specialties, workflows, chips, speed presets, report templates, V3 data layers.
- Validator evidence.
- Known limitations and review status.

### QA / Validator Evidence
- Clinical data validators.
- Speed preset validators.
- Export safety validator.
- Analytics safety validator.
- V3 data validators.
- Manual QA reports.

### Future AI Scribe Research Path
- Interest form only today.
- No audio recording in ClinicNote.
- Future Scribe requires separate privacy, consent, clinical safety, and institutional review.
- No claim that Scribe is available or approved.

### Pilot Readiness Checklist
- Clinical safety owner identified.
- Privacy/legal owner identified.
- De-identified demo script.
- No patient data demo package.
- Incident/feedback process.
- Rollback plan.
- Local policy review.
- Ethics or institutional review dependency assessed.

### No-Patient-Data Demo Package
- Fictional cases only.
- De-identified examples.
- Offline screenshots or local demo.
- Clear warning not to enter real patient data.

### Risk Mitigation Outline
- Misuse as diagnosis/treatment tool.
- Entry of patient identifiers.
- Overreliance on drafts.
- Misinterpretation of V3 prompts as recommendations.
- Calculator misuse.
- Institutional policy mismatch.

## Claims Not Allowed
- Hospital approval.
- Regulatory approval.
- NHS/NICE/DHA/MOHAP/MOH endorsement.
- Certified clinical decision support.
- Ready for real patient data.
- Automatic diagnosis.
- Treatment recommendations.
- Live consultation recording.

## Next Step
Use this plan only after internal self-review and private doctor testing produce safe, de-identified evidence. Do not use it as sales copy until reviewed.
